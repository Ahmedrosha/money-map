Money Map · Tamdeen Company

Need help, found a problem or have an idea? Email us at info@tamdeen-properties.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your phone model and the app version (Settings, at the bottom).

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. We never receive it, so we cannot recover it if the phone is lost or the app is deleted. Please keep a backup: connect Google Drive or Dropbox in Settings, or export a backup file to Google Drive, email or your computer. See all backup options.

Moving to a new phone

Connect the same Google Drive or Dropbox account on the new phone and restore from it, or restore the backup file you exported, from Settings → Backup.

Stock prices look old

Tap Refresh on the investment account. Egyptian stock prices are delayed about 15 minutes. You can always set a price yourself.

Bank SMS on iPhone

Money Map can save your bank's SMS so you can add them as transactions in one tap.

Automatically (iOS 16 or later):

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation → + → Message.
  2. Message Contains: EGP (you can also pick your bank as the sender). Choose Run Immediately, then Next.
  3. Tap New Blank Automation, search Money Map and tap Add Bank Message. Its Message is filled with the SMS automatically. Tap Done.

Each bank SMS now waits in Bank Messages the next time you open the app. Apple doesn't allow sharing automations, so this step is done once on each iPhone.

By hand (iOS 16 or later): get the Money Map shortcut, then in Messages long-press a bank SMS → Share → Money Map – Bank SMS.
