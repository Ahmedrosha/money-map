Money Map · Tamdeen Company

Need help, found a problem or have an idea? Email us at info@tamdeen-properties.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your phone model and the app version (Settings, at the bottom).

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Turn on Dropbox backup in Settings to keep a copy in your own Dropbox, or export a backup file.

Moving to a new phone

Connect the same Dropbox account on the new phone, or restore the backup file you exported, from Settings → Backup.

Stock prices look old

Tap Refresh on the investment account. Egyptian stock prices are delayed about 15 minutes. You can always set a price yourself.
