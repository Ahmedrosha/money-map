Everything you own and owe, in one app that stays on your phone. Unlimited accounts and transactions, free.

Accounts & net worth

Your whole financial picture on one screen

  • Bank accounts, cash, wallets, credit cards, loans, savings, certificates, gold, property, stocks and crypto
  • Net worth over time, in any currency, with live exchange rates
  • Cards due soon, backup status and your monthly summary at a glance
  • Money you've lent or borrowed, per person — call or WhatsApp them from the app
Transactions

Add spending in seconds

  • Type it, say it (“spent 450 on fuel”), or scan a receipt
  • Bank SMS turned into transactions — automatically on Android, with a Shortcut on iPhone
  • InstaPay fees worked out for you
  • Split one payment across categories or with a friend, tags for trips, receipt photos
  • Optional: remember where you paid, and see it on a map PRO
  • List or calendar view, search and filters, recurring payments and subscriptions
Investments

Your full investing story

  • EGX stocks and funds with prices about 15 minutes behind the market
  • Buy, sell and dividends — profit for each stock, including ones you've already sold
  • Import trades from your broker's invoices or screenshots
  • Crypto too: coin prices and Binance trade history import
  • Portfolio report against the EGX30 or Bitcoin PRO
Credit cards

Never miss a due date

  • Statements, closing and due dates, minimum payment and available limit
  • Installment plans, month by month
  • Points and cashback with your bank's own rules
  • Reminders before the due date, and pay from any account in one tap
Budgets & goals

Plan the month, save for what matters

  • Monthly budgets per category, with alerts before you go over
  • Savings goals linked to your accounts — see how much to put aside each month
  • A celebration when you reach a goal, and a card to share (no amounts shown)
Reports PRO

See where your money goes

  • Spending and income by category, budget vs actual, trends
  • Month summary and Year in Review
  • “Where I Spend” map of your places
  • Excel export of everything

And much more

🏠 Property & rent

Flats and land, by m², feddan or qirat — even a share with family. Rent income with yearly increases.

🪙 Gold

Gold by weight and karat, valued at today's price.

💱 Many currencies

Salary in one currency, savings in another — totals in the currency you choose.

🔒 App lock

Face ID or fingerprint, and a one-tap “hide amounts”.

📱 Widgets

Your numbers on the home screen.

🛟 Backups

Automatic daily backup on your phone, plus your own Google Drive or Dropbox.

🏆 Milestones

Celebrate net-worth milestones and share your Year in Review — percentages only.

🇪🇬 English & Arabic

The whole app in both languages.

Your data stays on your phone. Money Map has no servers and never receives your data, so keep a backup — see your backup options.

المعاملات

  • رسوم إنستاباي بتتحسب لوحدها
  • قسّم الدفعة على أكتر من فئة أو مع صاحبك، وتاجات للسفريات، وصور الإيصالات
  • اختياري: سجّل مكان الدفع وشوفه على الخريطة PRO
  • عرض قائمة أو تقويم، بحث وفلاتر، ومدفوعات متكررة واشتراكات
