Everything Money Map does

One app for your daily spending and your long-term wealth. Free to use, with Pro for reports.

Accounts and net worth

  • Bank accounts, cash, wallets, credit cards, loans, savings, certificates, gold, property, stocks and crypto
  • Net worth over time in any currency, with live exchange rates
  • Money you've lent or borrowed, per person, with call and WhatsApp from the app

Transactions

  • Type it, say it (“spent 450 on fuel”), or scan a receipt
  • Bank SMS into transactions: automatic on Android, with a Shortcut on iPhone (iOS 16 or later)
  • InstaPay fees worked out for you
  • Split a payment across categories or with a friend, tags, receipt photos
  • Recurring payments and subscriptions, list or calendar view
  • Optional: remember where you paid

Investments

  • EGX stocks and funds, prices about 15 minutes behind the market
  • Buy, sell and dividends, with profit for stocks you've already sold
  • Import trades from your broker's invoices or screenshots
  • Crypto prices and Binance trade import
  • Portfolio report against EGX30 or Bitcoin (Pro)

Credit cards

  • Statements, closing and due dates, minimum payment and available limit
  • Installment plans month by month
  • Points and cashback with your bank's rules
  • Reminders before the due date, and pay from any account

Budgets and goals

  • Monthly budgets per category, with alerts
  • Savings goals linked to your accounts, with what to save each month
  • Share a card when you reach a goal, with no amounts shown

Reports (Pro)

  • Spending and income by category, budget vs actual, trends
  • Month summary and Year in Review
  • “Where I Spend” map of your places
  • Excel export of everything

Why we built it

Money Map was built by someone who has tracked every pound for more than 12 years, using one money app after another.

Real life kept bringing situations those apps couldn't handle: credit cards with installments and points, InstaPay fees, a salary in one currency and savings in another, stocks bought and sold, gold, certificates, land shared with family, money lent to a friend.

So we built the app we always wanted: one that understands how money works in Egypt, keeps your data private on your phone, and never makes you pay to add an account or a transaction.

Money Map is made by Tamdeen Company in Egypt.
