Privacy Policy

Money Map · Tamdeen Company · Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short

Your financial data stays on your phone. We have no user accounts, no advertising and no tracking. We never see, collect, sell or share your financial data. Our server checks Pro purchases on Android and stores nothing for that (see Checking Pro purchases). If you use invites, it keeps a random phone ID and your invite code, never your name or money data (see Invite friends).

Data stored on your phone

Accounts, transactions, budgets, cards, investments, notes, tags, receipt photos and settings are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. A full card number, if you choose to save one, is kept in the phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, Keystore on Android). Deleting the app deletes this data unless you made a backup.

Backups you control

  • Dropbox (optional): if you connect Dropbox, the app uploads a copy of your data to your own Dropbox account so you can restore it or use it on another phone. Dropbox's privacy policy applies to that copy. You can disconnect at any time in the app.
  • Google Drive (optional): if you connect Google Drive, the app saves backup copies in a private app folder in your own Google Drive that only Money Map can see (it keeps the last 10). The app asks Google only for access to that folder, not to your other files. Google's privacy policy applies to those copies. You can disconnect at any time in the app.
  • Files you export (backups, Excel, PDF) go only where you choose to save or share them.

Optional phone features

The app asks for each permission only when you use the related feature, and you can refuse or turn it off in your phone's settings.

  • Camera and photos: to scan a receipt or a screenshot. The text is read on your phone.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to add a transaction by voice. On iPhone, speech may be processed by Apple, and on Android by your phone's speech service (Google), according to their privacy policies.
  • Contacts: to pick a payee from your contacts and call or WhatsApp them. Only the name and number you pick are saved in the app; your contacts are never uploaded.
  • Location (optional, off by default): only if you turn on “Remember Where I Spend” and tap “Add Location” on an expense. The place is saved with that expense on your phone. To show an area name, your phone's own map service (Apple or Google) looks up the coordinates. The app never reads your location in the background.
  • Notifications: for reminders such as card due dates and recurring payments.
  • Face ID / fingerprint: to lock the app. Handled by your phone; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Bank SMS (Android): only if you switch on “Read Bank SMS Automatically” and allow SMS access. The app then reads SMS messages only from the bank sender names you set in your accounts' details, to suggest and record your transactions, card statements and bills. All other messages are ignored. Messages are processed on your phone only; they are never uploaded to our servers or shared with anyone. You can switch this off any time in the app or in Android settings.
  • Bank notifications (Android): only if you switch on notification access. The app then reads notifications from your messages app, only from the bank senders you set in your accounts, to suggest transactions. It ignores every other notification, and what it reads stays on your phone and is never uploaded. You can switch this off any time in Android settings.

Information the app downloads

To show current values, the app downloads public market data. These requests contain only what is needed to look up a price (for example a currency code or a stock symbol) and no personal or financial data:

  • Exchange rates (open.er-api.com, currency-api via jsDelivr)
  • Egyptian stock prices (TradingView, Yahoo Finance)
  • Crypto prices (Binance, CryptoCompare)

The “Where I Spend” report shows a Google map; Google receives only the map area being viewed, never your expenses or amounts, under Google's privacy policy.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. The app's text recognition uses Google ML Kit on your device, which may send anonymous usage and performance information to Google.

Checking Pro purchases (Android)

To make sure a Money Map Pro purchase is genuine, the Android app sends our purchase server (hosted on Cloudflare) the purchase receipt Google Play gives the app, the product bought (Lifetime, monthly or yearly) and a Google Play Integrity token that shows the app was installed from Google Play. The server asks Google Play whether the purchase is valid and replies yes or no. This happens when you buy or restore Pro and at most once a day after that.

The server receives no name, email, phone number or financial data, and keeps nothing: the request is used only to answer and is then discarded. Google processes the receipt and the Integrity token under Google's privacy policy. No account or sign-in is needed. On iPhone, purchases are checked with Apple on your phone.

Invite friends

Invite friends gives each phone an invite code; friends who join with it and use the app for a week earn you free months of Pro. For this, the app sends our server (hosted on Cloudflare): a random ID for your phone (on Android made from the app's own phone ID, on iPhone created by the app), whether it is an Android phone or an iPhone, the invite code you entered, how many transactions you have saved (a number only, to confirm a friend really uses the app), whether you own Pro Lifetime, and a Google Play Integrity token (Android) or an Apple DeviceCheck token (iPhone) that shows the request comes from a real phone running the real app. Apple DeviceCheck also lets the server mark that this iPhone already used an invite code, so a code can't be used twice on one phone.

The server keeps the phone ID, its invite code, which code it joined with and when, the last day the app talked to the server (only the date, to count how many phones use the app), and the free months it earned (on iPhone, the Apple offer code given for each month). It never receives your name, email, phone number, contacts or any transaction, amount or account. This happens when you open Invite friends, when the app starts (at most a few times a day) and once a day while your own invite is waiting to count. Free months on iPhone are redeemed with Apple. To have your invite data deleted, email us your invite code (Settings > Help & About > Invite friends).

Your responsibility for backups

Because we never receive or store your data, we cannot restore it if your phone is lost, damaged or reset, or the app is deleted. You are responsible for keeping a backup, using the options in the app (automatic daily backup on the phone, Google Drive, Dropbox, or a backup file saved where you choose). See backup options.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything inside the app or by removing it. See how to delete your data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from them.
