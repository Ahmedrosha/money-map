Delete your data

Money Map · Tamdeen Company

In short

Money Map has no accounts and no servers. All your data is stored only on your phone, so you delete it yourself, in the app or by removing the app. We keep nothing, because we never receive it.

Option 1: delete inside the app

  1. Open Money Map and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Reset App.
  3. Choose Delete My Data (accounts, transactions, budgets, portfolios and recurring items; categories and settings stay) or Reset Everything (like a new install).
  4. Type RESET to confirm.

Option 2: delete the app

Uninstalling Money Map from your phone removes all the data it stored there, including receipt photos and any saved card number.

Backup copies you made

If you connected Dropbox or Google Drive, a backup copy exists in your own account. We cannot see or delete it, so remove it yourself:

  • Dropbox: open Dropbox, go to the Apps folder and delete the Money Map folder. You can also disconnect Dropbox inside Money Map.
  • Google Drive: open Google Drive, go to Settings, then Manage apps, choose Money Map and select Delete hidden app data.
  • Files you exported or saved (backup files, Excel, PDF) are wherever you saved them. Delete them there.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us. Any data on your phone is deleted when you follow the steps above. Store purchase records are held by Google Play or Apple under their own policies.
