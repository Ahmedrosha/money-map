Your data, your backup

Important: Money Map has no servers and never receives your data. Everything stays on your phone, which keeps it private, but it also means we cannot recover your data if your phone is lost, broken or reset, or the app is deleted. Keeping a backup is your responsibility.

Money Map gives you several ways to back up, and we recommend using at least one that keeps a copy off your phone:

  • Automatic daily backup (on by default): a copy on your phone every day, keeping the last 7 days and 4 weeks. Protects against mistakes, but not against losing the phone.
  • Google Drive (recommended): connect your Google account and the app backs up automatically to a private folder in your own Drive, keeping the last 10 copies.
  • Dropbox: connect your own Dropbox and every change is copied there automatically. Also moves your data to a new phone.
  • Back Up & Share: send a backup file to Google Drive, email, WhatsApp or anywhere you like.
  • Save to phone storage: save the file to Files / Downloads, or copy it to your computer.
  • iPhone: your iCloud backup of the phone normally includes the app's data too.

To restore, open the backup file with Money Map, or use Settings → Backup & Restore. Your current data is kept so you can undo.

Our story

Money Map was built by someone who has tracked every pound for more than 12 years, using one money app after another.

Over those years, real life kept bringing situations the apps couldn't handle: credit cards with installments and points, InstaPay fees, salaries in one currency and savings in another, stocks bought and sold, gold, certificates, a flat or a piece of land shared with family, money lent to a friend. Each time meant a workaround, a spreadsheet on the side, or giving up.

So we built the app we always wanted: one place that understands how money really works in Egypt, keeps your data private on your phone, and doesn't make you pay to add an account or a transaction.

Almost every feature in Money Map comes from a real situation we went through ourselves.
